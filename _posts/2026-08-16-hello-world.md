After a long time on Wix, I've finally moved this site to GitHub Pages with Jekyll. Here's why, and what's coming next.

## Why I left Wix

Wix is convenient, but it comes with a real cost — literally and creatively. The monthly fee was hard to justify for a personal site, and the editor made it hard to produce clean, semantic HTML. Every page felt heavier than it needed to be.

## Why Jekyll + GitHub Pages

The combination ticks every box for a personal site:

- **Free hosting** via GitHub Pages
- **Full control** over the HTML, CSS, and structure
- **Blog posts in Markdown** — no editor to fight with
- **Version-controlled everything** — every change is a commit

## What's coming

I plan to write here occasionally about design, front-end development, and things I'm working on. No schedule, no newsletter. Just a place to think out loud.
