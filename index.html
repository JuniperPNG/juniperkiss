PlantSciCV

Every bullet point
has a story behind it.

I made this site as a final-year undergraduate Plant Biology student with a few stories to tell. Research jobs, grants and awards look impressive on a CV — but behind each line there were chance meetings, applications filled out while living in a tent, random emails, and a lot of very good people.

Read the CV Read the blog University assignments

Not fitting into boxes

Two PhDs that did not finish, a tent for a house, a career that peaked early, and a lot of countries. I write it all down, because a tidy CV is a dishonest one.

See the CV, photographs and all

What is here

Three things, honestly told

  • The CV, with the stories

    The full work history, education, awards and presentations — annotated with what actually happened. Including the parts that did not go to plan.

  • The blog

    How I raised £40,000 from grants and awards, living with farmers in Nepal, and how I developed a passion for invasive brambles.

  • University assignments

    Real undergraduate essays with their marks and the marker's feedback, so international students and lecturers can see what a UK grade looks like.

Who this is for

Undergraduates, graduate students, and the people who teach them

  • Undergraduates who either do, or very much do not, know where they want to end up.
  • Graduate students who need a reminder to laugh when an experiment or a presentation does not go to plan.
  • Professors, researchers and employers now teaching, supervising and hiring my generation of scientists.

Behind the fun and unusual stories, I also try to be honest about the sacrifices, and about how world events shape a young scientist's motivation. I share my university assignments with their marks so that international students and educators can see what an essay scoring above 90% in the UK system actually looks like.
