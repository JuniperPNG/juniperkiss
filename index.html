About this website and Juniper (troublesome me!)

I first built my website as a final-year undergraduate Plant Biology student with a few stories to tell. Research jobs, grants and awards look impressive on a CV — but behind each line there were chance meetings, applications filled out while living in a tent, random emails, and a lot of amazing humans.

The stories include how I ended up in Plant Biology after three years of studying Marine Biology part-time, how I raised £40,000 from grants and awards, how I lived with farmers in Nepal, and how I developed a passion for invasive brambles. I also try to explain why I once had 70 fish in a suitcase and two sharks on my back on the London Underground, and why I was once in a dark forest with metre-long shears and red stains on my clothes. Every year, I seem to lose everything, rebuild it all, get everything hoped for – only until I lose it all again. It's a rollercoaster. That I happen to love.

To my continuous surprise, people seem to believe in troublesome me. I have so many ridiculous Juniper-stories and I laugh at myself a lot. I hope that sharing my nerdy ridiculousness makes others laugh too!

Thank you for reading!
